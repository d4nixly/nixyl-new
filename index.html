About NIXYL

WHY I CREATED NIXYL

It started with a problem that probably sounds familiar.

I would open Roblox, scroll through games, and think:

“What am I even going to play?”

There are thousands of experiences on Roblox. Sometimes I wanted something chaotic with friends. Sometimes I only had a few minutes. Sometimes I wanted something completely new.

But finding the right game could take longer than actually playing one.

Then one day, I was playing Roblox when my mom told me I only had 30 minutes left to play.

Thirty minutes.

That sounds like enough time to play something — until you spend ten of those minutes scrolling through games, opening experiences, reading descriptions, and trying to decide what is actually worth your time.

And that's when I started thinking:

What if you could just tell something what you wanted to play?

Something like:

“I have 30 minutes. I'm playing with two friends. I want something funny.”

And instead of scrolling endlessly, you would immediately get experiences that actually fit.

That idea became the beginning of NIXYL.

I wanted NIXYL to be more than another list of Roblox games. I wanted it to become a place where players could discover experiences based on their time, their mood, and the people they're playing with.

But I also wanted to build something for the other side of Roblox.

I'm a Roblox developer myself, and I've spent time looking for plugins, scripts, tutorials, tools, and resources while building things. Sometimes finding the right developer resource can be just as frustrating as finding the right game.

So NIXYL became two things:

A place to discover what to play.
And a place to discover what to build with.

NIXYL is still growing, but the idea behind it is simple:

You shouldn't have to spend your entire free time figuring out what to do with it.

Whether you have five minutes, thirty minutes, or an entire evening, NIXYL is here to help you find something worth your time.

— Daniel
Founder & Roblox Developer
